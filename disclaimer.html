Scope, sources and rights

Disclaimer.

The purpose of this website is to explore typographic and layout styles for specialised tourist visits, as an end-of-course project for the Information Modeling and Web Technologies course of the Master’s Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna, under the supervision of Prof. Fabio Vitali.

Educational purpose

The descriptions and metadata are presented for research, teaching and project-prototyping. They are not a substitute for the original films, television series, official venue information or rights-holder publications.

Filming-location facts were cross-checked against the sources listed below. Opening hours, access rules, transit conditions and property status can change; visitors must verify current information and respect all public/private boundaries.

Images

All bundled location images are original SVG shot-guide illustrations made for this prototype. They do not reproduce copyrighted film frames. QR codes and data visualisations are generated for this project.

Selected sources

Location facts, addresses and behind-the-scenes details were drawn primarily from seeing-stars.com's dedicated La La Land location pages, cross-checked against general reference sources for each real venue.

  • https://www.seeing-stars.com/Locations/LaLaLand/QuickIndex.shtml (index of all location pages used)
  • https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_La_Land
  • https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griffith_Observatory
  • https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Central_Market
  • https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Street_Bridge
  • https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Studios,_Burbank
  • https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2017/02/la-la-land-production-design

Note on this list: the exact seeing-stars.com page URL for each individual location wasn't captured while compiling this dataset — only the index page and pasted page text were used. Before submission, revisit the index and add the specific per-location page link to each entry's source list in data/locations.json.
