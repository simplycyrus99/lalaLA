Scope, sources and rights

Disclaimer.

The purpose of this website is to explore typographic and layout styles for specialised tourist visits, as an end-of-course project for the Information Modeling and Web Technologies course of the Master’s Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna, under the supervision of Prof. Fabio Vitali.

Educational purpose

The descriptions and metadata are presented for research, teaching and project-prototyping. They are not a substitute for the original films, television series, official venue information or rights-holder publications.

Filming-location facts were cross-checked against the sources listed below. Opening hours, access rules, transit conditions and property status can change; visitors must verify current information and respect all public/private boundaries.
