About the project

La La Land's Los Angeles, as a distributed museum.

La La L.A. was designed as a companion for planning at home and navigating in the city. It treats each of a single film's real filming locations as a museum item with an exact geographic position, a camera orientation, structured metadata and audience-sensitive interpretation.

Team fields are intentionally editable.

Information Modeling and Web Technologies
Master's Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge

What is complete

15 locations from La La Land (2016), two narratives, two switchable graphic themes, nine reading combinations per item, a self-contained geographic map, QR codes, and CSV/JSON data.

What to personalise

Team identity, GitHub Pages URL, your own field photographs in each location's photo gallery, any professor-requested bibliography format, and final checking of access conditions before an actual visit.
